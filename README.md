# The Leap

A 90-minute synthesis session for people who have been thinking about a change for years and have not moved.

The skill is free. Built and used by [Cy Law](https://breakingcycles.life), ex-corporate, on the road since 2024, building Breaking Cycles in public. Same framework I use when I run live sessions for paying clients.

If you want to run it with me instead, [details on the site](https://breakingcycles.life/the-leap).

---

## What it is

A guided conversation that does one thing: helps you figure out what life you actually want to build, what could fund it, and what your first real move is. In one session, not six months of reading.

Not a productivity tool. Not a business plan generator. The thing that comes before both. A clarity conversation for someone standing at the edge of their conventional life, not sure if they can jump.

It works through what you already know but cannot quite say out loud. What you are running from, what you are running toward. What you have been avoiding. What you have actually tried and what stopped you. Where your real advantage is, drawn from your specific life, not a template.

The result is a one-page read on you. 2 to 3 directions you could actually take, in your own language, sharpened against what is real out there. A first move for each. No 40-page business plan. No vision board. Something you can act on this week.

---

## What you get

A ~75-minute conversation with Claude. At the end, a short read-back of what came up, then a full written report. 2000 to 3000 words, 2 to 3 directions, each with a first move you can try this week.

The value is recognition plus sharpening, not surprise. The directions you walk away with are familiar, drawn from your own material, but more concrete and validated than you could articulate alone.

## How to install

You need Claude Desktop with skill support enabled.

1. Download `theleap.md` from this folder.
2. In Claude Desktop, go to **Settings → Skills**.
3. Click **Add skill**, select `theleap.md`.
4. Open a new conversation.
5. Type `@leap` to load the skill.

The skill runs you through the full flow. Honesty is the only thing that makes it work.

---

## Voice

The skill is built in a specific voice. Direct, dry, slightly self-deprecating, no motivational language, no guru tone. If you read the questions and they feel flat, that is the point. The flatness is what lets the real answers come out.

---

## What this is not

- Not a coaching programme
- Not a course
- Not a community
- Not a substitute for therapy
- Not a guarantee that you will know what to do at the end

It is a question framework. The work after is yours.
